Our Story

Actually, we are the Boys at the Back. Since this is the second website we have created as a group, we wanted to give this project a name that connects to its purpose. Because our website focuses on books and online book ordering, we decided to change our original group name into BooksAtTheBack. We kept the identity of our group while giving it a name that represents our bookstore. As IT students, we created this website to apply our knowledge, improve our skills, and challenge ourselves to build something useful. Our goal is to provide a simple and convenient platform where people can browse books, place orders, and discover new learning materials. This project represents our teamwork, creativity, and continuous learning as aspiring IT professionals. From Boys at the Back to BooksAtTheBack — our second website, built with teamwork and a love for learning.

Our Mission
